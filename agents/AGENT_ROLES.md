# Research Agent Roles

Agents are evidence-specialists. They do not independently declare final research gaps.

1. Foundations and terminology
2. Architectures and reference models
3. XR communication, rendering, and HCI
4. 5G/6G/B5G networking
5. Edge, fog, cloud, and cloud continuum
6. Digital twins and cyber-physical integration
7. AI and machine learning
8. Generative AI and autonomous agents
9. Resource allocation, scheduling, and offloading
10. Semantic communications
11. Security and resilience
12. Privacy, identity, authentication, and trust
13. Blockchain and Web3
14. Interoperability and standards
15. QoS, QoE, scalability, and reliability
16. Energy efficiency and sustainability
17. Applications and deployment evidence
18. Algorithms, datasets, and benchmark inventory
19. Reproducibility and open-artifact audit
20. Claim–evidence audit
21. Contradiction and trade-off mining
22. Cross-layer gap mining
23. Closest-review comparison
24. Target-journal coverage and tutorial-value audit

All agents write into a common evidence schema so that results can be deduplicated and cross-checked.
