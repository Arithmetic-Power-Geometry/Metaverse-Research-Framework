## Verified evidence added
Seven complete records were appended to the master BibTeX, covering DT freshness, DT-assisted 6G industrial resource allocation, semantic-enhanced industrial architecture, DT portability/interoperability, a DT-Metaverse review, cross-modal GenAI semantic communication, and dataset-backed semantic interpretation.

## Key synthesis finding
The comparability problem becomes stronger outside XR resource allocation. Digital-twin work may optimize freshness/synchronization or industrial task success, whereas semantic-communication work may optimize task/semantic fidelity, change detection, energy or multimodal reconstruction.

This means the manuscript needs a **metric ontology** before it can make cross-domain evidence statements.

## New proposed metric classes
1. transport/network: latency, rate, reliability, packet/delivery success;
2. compute/resource: CPU/GPU/bandwidth use, utilization, cost;
3. immersive QoE: resolution, visual quality, MTP, experience/fairness;
4. synchronization/freshness: AoI, DT/model freshness, desynchronization;
5. learning/task: accuracy, completion time, convergence;
6. semantic: task success, semantic fidelity, reconstruction/change detection;
7. energy/sustainability: device/system energy, energy efficiency, carbon where measured;
8. security/trust: disruption risk, attack success, trust/consistency;
9. deployment: scale, availability, prototype/pilot/operational maturity.
