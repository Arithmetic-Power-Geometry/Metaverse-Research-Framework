## Evidence consequence
Semantic communication is not merely a future-direction label in the Metaverse literature. Existing work includes concrete stochastic optimization, incentive/auction mechanisms, contest-theoretic allocation, AIGC integration, real-data-supported simulation and sustainability-oriented visual-semantic architectures. Any manuscript gap must therefore target comparability, evidence maturity, artifact reproducibility, deployment or unresolved cross-layer trade-offs rather than 'semantic communication is unexplored'.
