## Historical synthesis
The technical ancestry of networked Metaverse systems predates the widespread use of the term Metaverse. By 2019, XR work already included viewport-adaptive 360-degree streaming prototypes and edge offloading/pre-rendering motivated by power and motion-to-photon latency. Later Metaverse systems inherit and combine these mechanisms with AI, digital twins, semantic communication, 5G/6G, security and distributed resource allocation.
