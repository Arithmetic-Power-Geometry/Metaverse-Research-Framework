## Evidence rule
Sustainability evidence must distinguish:
1. conceptual sustainability claims;
2. literature-review evidence;
3. prospective/scenario modeling;
4. measured energy/resource utilization;
5. life-cycle assessment;
6. measured carbon/emissions;
7. operational deployment outcomes.

Modeled avoided emissions cannot be reported as measured operational carbon savings.

## Benchmark rule
MetaverseBench and XRBench already establish benchmark precedents. The manuscript cannot claim first Metaverse/XR benchmark. Our contribution is cross-paper evidence normalization and reproducibility/comparability auditing.
