# RUN 02 — Closest-Review Saturation, Batch 01

## Purpose
Attack the proposed COMST novelty by finding surveys that already cover the same scope, architecture, algorithms, evidence maturity, reproducibility, deployment, or gap analysis.

## New high-risk competitors

### R016 — Networked Metaverse Systems: Foundations, Gaps, Research Directions
IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society (2024), DOI 10.1109/OJCOMS.2024.3426098.

This is the most important new overlap found in this batch. It already frames the Metaverse from a technical networked-systems perspective and covers enabling technologies, system architectures, communication performance, mobility, large-scale operation, end-system architecture, gaps and research directions.

**Consequence:** "networked Metaverse + foundations + architectures + gaps" cannot be our novelty claim.

### R017 — Metaverse Architectures
A 2025 systematic review reports 103 peer-reviewed studies, 33 definitions and 25 architectural models.

**Consequence:** a unified definition/architecture survey is not enough.

### R019 — Transportation data-driven SLR
A 2025 domain review screened 1589 records and retained 101, and quantified simulation/testbed/pilot/performance-evidence maturity.

**Consequence:** evidence-maturity analysis is not automatically novel. Our stronger claim must be cross-domain technical normalization connecting problem, algorithm, workload, baseline, metric, environment, reproducibility and deployment, with contrary-evidence gap validation.

### R020 — Industrial Metaverse COMST survey
A 2026 IEEE COMST survey is reported for Industrial Metaverse enabling technologies, open problems and future trends.

**Consequence:** industrial enabling-technology/open-problem synthesis is already occupied within the target journal.

## Candidate novelty after Batch 01

The safest current differentiator is NOT:
- first networked Metaverse survey;
- first architecture survey;
- first resource-allocation benchmark;
- first evidence-maturity analysis;
- first survey of gaps/open problems.

The candidate contribution that still requires saturation testing is:

> A cross-domain, communications-centric evidence synthesis that links problem → method/algorithm → workload/dataset → baseline → metric → experimental environment → evidence maturity → reproducibility → deployment, and validates candidate gaps through explicit contrary-evidence search.

Do not use "first" yet.
