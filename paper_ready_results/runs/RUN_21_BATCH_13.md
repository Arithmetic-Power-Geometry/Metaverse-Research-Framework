## Synthesis consequence
Viewport-adaptive immersive delivery evolves from probabilistic/white-box prediction and tile scheduling toward cross-user learning, GNN/meta-learning, gaze-aware prediction, privacy protection and mobile-friendly distributed inference. Therefore, the manuscript should present this as an evolving technical lineage rather than a collection of isolated Metaverse algorithms.

## Cross-layer insight
Privacy is not only an identity/security-layer issue. Gaze and viewport traces used for immersive QoE optimization are themselves privacy-sensitive behavioral data. This creates a concrete QoE–privacy–bandwidth trade-off suitable for the cross-layer section.
