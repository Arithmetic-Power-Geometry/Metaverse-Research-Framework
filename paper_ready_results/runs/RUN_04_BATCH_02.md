## Comparative evidence finding
The primary studies do not share one common optimization problem. Their decision spaces include asynchronous UL/DL offloading/channel/power control, QoS-aware channel allocation, heterogeneous 360-video adaptation, DT synchronization/resource allocation, joint compute-bandwidth-resolution control, and metaheuristic end-edge allocation.

Therefore a table that simply reports 'algorithm A beats algorithm B' across papers would be methodologically invalid unless P-W-D-H-N-B-M-E compatibility is established.

## Novelty implication
The 2024 Networked Metaverse Systems survey already treats communication performance, mobility, scale and end-system architecture as explicit gap areas. Our added value must operate below that level: measurement provenance, comparability, artifact reproducibility, evidence maturity and falsification-tested gap status.
