## Main finding
High IDS accuracy on CIC-IDS2017, CICIoT2023, UNSW-NB15 or similar generic network/IoT benchmarks is evidence of classifier performance under that dataset distribution, not direct evidence of operational Metaverse security. Dataset provenance, attack coverage, topology, traffic generation, class balance and deployment shift must remain visible.

## Authentication finding
Metaverse authentication evidence already spans ECC three-factor schemes, quantum/blockchain/DID approaches, anonymous avatar credentials with traceability, blockchain space authentication, PRIDA-ME, MetaL and post-quantum protocols. Generic authentication novelty is occupied.
