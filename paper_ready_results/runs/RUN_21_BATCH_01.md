# RUN 21 — Corpus Saturation and Bibliography Expansion Batch 01

## Workflow safety
The live workflow and requirements were re-fetched before this batch. No CI workflow or scientific benchmark was executed.

## Saturation method
This batch used forward/backward snowballing from high-overlap COMST/edge/security references, then DOI/title deduplication against the live master bibliography. Records were admitted only when authors, title, venue, year and persistent identifier were resolved; journal volume/issue/pages were included where verified.
