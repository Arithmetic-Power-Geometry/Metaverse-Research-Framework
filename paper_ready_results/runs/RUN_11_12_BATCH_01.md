## Workflow safety
The workflow and requirements were re-fetched before this run. The workflow still invokes evidence validation, parameter-provenance validation and the PNE017 fidelity gate. No workflow or unresolved benchmark was manually executed.

## Main finding
Interoperability cannot be described simply as an unstandardized open problem.

Verified normative evidence includes IEEE 3812.1-2023 for Metaverse identity requirements; ITU-T Y.4239 for a digital-twin integration reference model; Y.4240 for interoperability interfaces; H.770.1 for cross-platform avatar, asset, content and identity scenarios/requirements; and H.770.2 for high-level cross-platform architecture.

A 2025 IEEE Engineering Management Review systematic review analyzes 74 interoperability publications across technological, data, human and institutional dimensions.

## Evidence rule
Standard existence is distinct from implementation evidence, conformance evidence, adoption evidence and operational deployment.
