Safety: live workflow, requirements and 168-record master bibliography were checked before writes. No CI workflow or benchmark was executed.

- Corbillon, De Simone, Simon 2017, 360-Degree Video Head Movement Dataset, MMSys, DOI 10.1145/3083187.3083215.
- Kreß, Lappe, Bremmer 2024, Comprehensive VR dataset for machine learning, Data in Brief 57:111187, DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2024.111187.
- Rubow et al. 2024, paired head/eye movement dataset, Scientific Data 11:1328, DOI 10.1038/s41597-024-04184-1.
- Xue et al. 2023, CEAP-360VR, IEEE TMM 25:243-255, DOI 10.1109/TMM.2021.3124080.

Finding: reusable immersive evidence predates Metaverse terminology and includes public head/viewport traces, synchronized content/sensor data, eye/head trajectories, physiological/behavioral annotations and processing code. Dataset existence is therefore not a novelty claim.

Artifact rule: public data, code, persistent archival identifier, executable instructions and independent reuse are separate fields.
