# Figure F01 — Evidence Maturity Ladder

Paper-ready figure specification.

E0 Concept/vision
→ E1 Architecture/analytical model
→ E2 Algorithm
→ E3 Simulation
→ E4 Prototype/testbed
→ E5 Public/curated dataset evaluation
→ E6 Operational deployment
→ E7 Independent reproduction
→ E8 Normalized cross-system benchmark

Important: this is an evidence-maturity axis, not a scientific-quality ranking. A study may be strong scientifically while occupying an earlier evidence tier.
