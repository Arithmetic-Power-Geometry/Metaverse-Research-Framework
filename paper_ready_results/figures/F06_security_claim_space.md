# Figure F06 — Security Claim Space
Property × Evidence × Scope × Normative maturity.

Property: authentication, authorization, confidentiality, integrity, privacy, availability, accountability.
Evidence: proposal, formal analysis, simulation, prototype, adversarial evaluation, conformance, reproduction, deployment.
Scope: user/avatar, device, platform, asset, digital twin, network/service, cross-platform.
Normative maturity: none, work item, final standard, implementation, conformance, adoption.
