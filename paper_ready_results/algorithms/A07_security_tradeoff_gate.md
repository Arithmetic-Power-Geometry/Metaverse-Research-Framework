# Algorithm A07 — Security–Usability Trade-off Gate
1. Identify security/privacy property.
2. Identify technical protection mechanism.
3. Record attack/security validation.
4. Record latency, compute, storage or communication overhead.
5. Record user-facing burden and interaction changes.
6. Record usability/trust/privacy outcomes when human subjects are involved.
7. Do not call a mechanism superior if security gains and usability costs are measured on incompatible axes.
8. Report Pareto/trade-off relation when possible.
9. Separate cryptographic validity from deployability and user acceptance.
