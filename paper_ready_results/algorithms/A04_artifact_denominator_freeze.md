# Algorithm A04 — Eligible Artifact Denominator Freeze

Input: saturated corpus S.

1. Assign each record a study type before artifact scoring.
2. Include evaluated primary algorithm/system studies in the primary denominator.
3. Tag benchmark papers and operational field studies explicitly.
4. Exclude secondary surveys/reviews, standards and dataset-only records from primary-study prevalence.
5. Keep conceptual architectures in a separate denominator unless executable evaluation exists.
6. Resolve duplicate publications and extended versions before counting.
7. Freeze the eligible IDs and corpus date.
8. Only then compute reproducibility numerators and percentages.
9. Report numerator, denominator, study-unit definition, audit date and unresolved-status policy with every prevalence statement.

Output: frozen eligible-ID ledger and defensible denominator.
