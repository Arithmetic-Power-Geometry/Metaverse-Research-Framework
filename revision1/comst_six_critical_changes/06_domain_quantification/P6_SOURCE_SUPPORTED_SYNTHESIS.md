# P6 source-supported synthesis update — 2026-10-06

This update records quantitative facts that can be supported directly from accessible primary/review sources. It does not convert overlapping metadata candidate sets into prevalence denominators.

## Network/resource allocation
A 2024 Computer Science Review survey compares 35 resource-allocation strategies and benchmarks 19 algorithms. This directly rejects a broad claim that comparative algorithm benchmarking is absent. The remaining evidence question is matched workload/baseline/metric reproducibility across studies.

## Security/privacy
The 2025 open-access systematic review covers 2013-2024 and reports predominant use of benchmarking, human experimentation and qualitative methods. It identifies residual weaknesses in policy compliance, accessibility, interoperability and back-end infrastructure security. Therefore the final paper should not claim that security evaluation is generally absent.

## Sustainability
A 2024 Journal of Cleaner Production review analyzed 66 studies using PRISMA. Sustainability is therefore an established literature. The defensible technical residual gap remains the distinction among proxy/modelled energy, simulated energy, directly measured joules, carbon accounting and life-cycle assessment.

## AI
The 2023 EAAI survey establishes a broad AI literature spanning ML/DL, NLP, vision, blockchain, networking, digital twins and neural interfaces. The residual gap should be framed as evaluation comparability, artifacts, transfer and operational evidence rather than absence of AI methods.

## Interoperability
The 2025 IEEE Engineering Management Review study identified 74 relevant publications from WoS/Scopus and reports lack of a standardized interoperability definition. This supports a standards/conformance/adoption gap, not a claim that interoperability research is absent.

## Rule
These source-supported counts describe the cited reviews' own corpora. They are not substituted for the present survey's P6 eligible denominators.
