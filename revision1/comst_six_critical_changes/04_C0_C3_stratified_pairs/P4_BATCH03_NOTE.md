# Frozen network batch 03 — source audit

Records covered: PNE012, PNE013, PNE014, PNE015, PNE018 and PNE019.

## Evidence policy
- Publisher abstracts/full article text and open preprints support problem, mechanism, evaluation and metric coding.
- An author-uploaded PDF or arXiv manuscript is not counted as executable code.
- "Software" in an author-contribution statement is not evidence of a public artifact.
- Failure to locate a repository is recorded as NOT_IDENTIFIED_IN_AUDITED_SOURCES, not ABSENT.
- Unknown reusable datasets, exact physical hardware equivalence, seeds and dependency manifests remain unresolved.
- P4 pair classes are conditional evidence judgments; no missing dimension is silently converted to zero.

## Batch result
The block supplies five conditionally comparable C2 pairs and one deliberately incompatible C0 negative control. It also broadens the network evidence matrix beyond RL-only studies by including auction/bargaining and NUMA-aware truthful mechanisms.
