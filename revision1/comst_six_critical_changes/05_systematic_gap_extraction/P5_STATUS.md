# P5 status — systematic prior-survey gap extraction

The closest-review denominator is frozen at 15 verified review records (R001-R015), spanning broad Metaverse, communications, interoperability, applications/software engineering, security/privacy, distributed ML, sustainability, resource allocation, AI, blockchain, fault management, digital twins and applied XR.

The repository previously contained a protocol but an empty gap-statement corpus. This revision adds the explicit 15-review extraction census.

A defensible gap statement requires the actual survey text and source location. Metadata descriptions such as "challenges" or "future directions" are not coded as gap claims. Therefore the source-by-source extraction cannot be declared complete until the 15 full texts are available/inspected.

Existing contrary-evidence work remains valid for propositions already explicitly registered, but it is not relabeled as a systematic extraction of all gap statements from these 15 surveys.
