# Deployment Claim Rules

## Terminology

Use the strongest term justified by evidence:

- simulated — D1
- prototyped — D2
- testbed-evaluated — D3
- piloted — D4
- operationally deployed — D5
- independently/multi-site replicated — D6

Do not call D1–D3 systems "real-world deployed" without additional evidence.

## Human-study rule

Record:

- participant count and population;
- study duration;
- task/context;
- comparison/control where applicable;
- objective and subjective measures;
- adverse effects/dropout where reported;
- ethics/consent information when available and relevant.

## Sustainability claim rule

When a paper reports energy/carbon/sustainability:

1. identify the system boundary;
2. distinguish measurement from analytical/model estimate;
3. record hardware/workload;
4. record geographic/temporal assumptions for carbon where available;
5. distinguish operational from embodied impact;
6. do not extrapolate beyond the reported boundary.

## Evidence synthesis

Deployment maturity and sustainability are reported by capability/problem family rather than as one field-wide average when coverage is heterogeneous.
