# Research Chronology

The project follows a fixed provenance order.

## Stage 1 — Concept and scope

Define the research need, target venue, review questions, evidence model, and reproducibility requirements.

## Stage 2 — Literature evidence

Execute searches, screening, snowballing, bibliographic verification, extraction, quality assessment, and closest-review comparison.

## Stage 3 — Taxonomy and gap validation

Construct taxonomies from evidence. Identify candidate gaps and test them against newer, older, contrary, standards, implementation, and benchmark evidence.

## Stage 4 — Benchmark selection

Select only problem families with sufficient comparability and research value. Freeze datasets/workloads, baselines, metrics, environments, and protocols.

## Stage 5 — Software implementation

Implement or adapt reference methods. Validate implementations before comparative experiments.

## Stage 6 — Experimental evidence

Run controlled experiments, repeated trials where appropriate, ablations/sensitivity analyses, and robustness checks.

## Stage 7 — New method, if warranted

Design a new algorithm only when Stage 6 demonstrates a specific unresolved limitation.

## Stage 8 — Automated artifacts

CI workflows reproduce machine-readable results and derive figures, tables, and benchmark summaries.

## Stage 9 — Evidence freeze

Freeze a versioned evidence release and document the search cutoff, software commit, environment, and artifact hashes.

## Stage 10 — Scholarly synthesis

Only after the evidence freeze is the paper assembled around the validated literature and experimental findings.

The repository documents the research process and artifacts; it is not treated as a manuscript-generation system.
