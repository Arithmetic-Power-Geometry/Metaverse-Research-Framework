# Dissection of the 2024 Resource-Allocation Benchmark

## Established facts

The 2024 Computer Science Review article is a direct closest-work constraint. It reviews 35 resource-allocation strategies and reports a benchmark involving 19 algorithms.

Accordingly, this project does not claim the first Metaverse resource-allocation taxonomy, comparative algorithm analysis, or benchmark.

## Information still required directly from the benchmark section

Exact names/citations of all 19 algorithms; algorithm-to-problem mapping; system/network/workload/compute/channel models; objectives and constraints; metric definitions and units; parameterization; repetitions/seeds; uncertainty/statistics; software/simulator; hardware; and code/data/supplement availability.

## Post-2024 dimensions to test against the benchmark

Federated-learning accuracy/update cost; anti-jamming conditions; O-RAN slicing/xAPP decisions; explicit GPU/CPU/bandwidth leasing; safe RL under motion-to-photon and queue constraints; aerial MEC; and online/economic mechanism design.

These are candidate extension dimensions, not validated gaps.

## Decision rule

A dimension becomes a benchmark-extension opportunity only when it is technically material, supported by peer-reviewed primary evidence, not adequately represented by the existing benchmark, reproducible baselines are available, and a common protocol can be specified without distorting the original problems.

Until then its status remains unresolved.
