# AI, Generative AI, and Autonomous-Agent Hypotheses

Status: candidate hypotheses only.

## AIH-01 — Generation quality is not system utility

Generative models may improve visual/textual/3D content quality while increasing inference latency, edge/cloud compute, bandwidth, energy, or interaction delay. Test whether generation studies jointly measure output quality and systems cost under interactive constraints.

## AIH-02 — Autonomous-agent evaluation lacks Metaverse-specific continuity metrics

Persistent agents may require identity, memory, goals, permissions, embodiment, and social context to remain coherent across sessions/worlds. Test whether current agent evaluations measure these continuity properties.

## AIH-03 — Digital-human realism and identity integrity are evaluated separately

Avatar/digital-human studies may optimize realism while security/privacy studies address impersonation, biometric leakage, or identity. Test whether these properties are jointly evaluated.

## AIH-04 — AI resource control and AI content generation compete for the same edge budget

Network/resource-management AI consumes compute to optimize the system while generative/perceptual AI consumes compute to deliver the experience. Test whether resource-allocation studies model AI inference/training workload itself as a first-class demand.

## AIH-05 — Foundation-model communication cost is under-integrated

Distributed/agentic/foundation-model systems can exchange prompts, embeddings, model updates, retrieved context, tool results, or multimodal state. Test whether Metaverse network models account for these traffic types rather than conventional content/task traffic alone.

## AIH-06 — Agent portability extends interoperability beyond assets

A portable intelligent actor may require transfer of identity, model/version, memory, tool capabilities, permissions, provenance, and policy constraints. Test whether interoperability standards and research address this compound state.

## AIH-07 — Safety evaluation is disconnected from QoE and latency

Guardrails/moderation may alter response time, availability, creativity, or interaction quality. Test whether safety and experiential/system metrics are jointly reported.

## AIH-08 — Human–agent evidence may lag technical agent capability

Test whether user studies, longitudinal interaction evidence, accessibility, trust calibration, and behavioral outcomes keep pace with increasingly capable virtual agents.
