# Network/Edge Optimization Evolution — Seed Timeline

This timeline records the first verified primary-study seed set. It is not yet the complete literature chronology.

## 2023 — Wireless decisions and experience-aware resource allocation

- Asynchronous hybrid RL addresses latency/reliability optimization in wireless Metaverse systems.
- DRL-based MEC/Web3 work introduces accumulated multi-user socialization experience into channel-resource allocation.
- Differentiated RL is applied to heterogeneous 360-degree video delivery.
- An enhanced whale optimization approach addresses collaborative end-edge VR tasks with energy/latency objectives.

## 2024 — Digital-twin synchronization becomes part of the resource-allocation problem

- Bidirectional DT–MEC work emphasizes that edge decisions and digital-twin construction/synchronization influence each other.
- FPER-CDDPG explicitly addresses time-varying DT updates, computing/bandwidth allocation, adaptive resolution, QoE, successful delivery, and long-term execution.

## 2025 — Collaborative cloud–edge–end caching

- GCNAC combines trajectory/popularity prediction, graph learning, and actor-critic reinforcement learning for collaborative Metaverse caching.

## 2026 — Hierarchical and increasingly joint decision problems

- MROA uses meta-reinforcement learning for computation offloading and resource allocation while distinguishing scene-rendering and avatar-interaction requirements.
- A frontier preprint jointly models user association, task offloading, UL/DL decisions, power allocation, in-network computing, MEC, game-theoretic equilibrium, and multi-agent RL.

## Candidate pattern to test

The technical problem appears to be expanding from isolated resource decisions toward increasingly coupled, dynamic, cross-layer decisions. The review must test whether evaluation methodology has expanded at the same rate.

A possible evidence gap is therefore:

> system-model complexity may be increasing faster than benchmark standardization, reproducibility, and cross-paper comparability.

This is a hypothesis for systematic testing, not a confirmed research gap.
