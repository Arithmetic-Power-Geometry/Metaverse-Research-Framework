# Sustainability and Deployment Hypotheses

Status: candidate hypotheses only.

## SDH-01 — Efficiency is often treated as sustainability

Test whether studies label reduced latency, bandwidth, or energy as "sustainable" without measuring broader operational carbon, embodied impacts, or rebound effects.

## SDH-02 — AI-enabled quality gains may externalize resource cost

Test whether GenAI, perception, semantic models, and intelligent controllers report the device/edge/cloud/network resources required to obtain QoE or task-quality gains.

## SDH-03 — Simulation maturity exceeds deployment maturity

Test the distribution of D0–D6 evidence across technical domains. Determine which capabilities have operational evidence versus predominantly simulation/prototype evidence.

## SDH-04 — Cross-layer energy accounting is incomplete

A method can reduce device energy while increasing edge/cloud/network energy. Test whether studies define system boundaries and jointly account for multiple energy locations.

## SDH-05 — Carbon claims lack temporal/geographic context

Operational carbon depends on electricity mix, time, location, utilization, and infrastructure efficiency. Test whether Metaverse carbon studies report these assumptions.

## SDH-06 — Dataset/testbed fragmentation blocks fair comparison

Test whether high-density algorithm families rely on study-specific synthetic workloads rather than shared datasets, traces, scenarios, and testbeds.

## SDH-07 — Human evidence is not aligned with systems evidence

QoE may be modeled analytically while real users experience cybersickness, accessibility barriers, fatigue, social effects, or interaction failures. Test where objective system metrics have corresponding human-subject evidence.
