# Initial Source Register

This register records the first verified closest-review set. It is not the final corpus.

## Broad baseline

- Wang et al. (2023), *A Survey on the Metaverse: The State-of-the-Art, Technologies, Applications, and Challenges*, IEEE Internet of Things Journal. DOI: 10.1109/JIOT.2023.3278329.
- Far et al. (2023), *Toward Metaverse of everything: Opportunities, challenges, and future directions of the next generation of visual/virtual communications*, Journal of Network and Computer Applications. DOI: 10.1016/j.jnca.2023.103675.

## Focused systematic/review baselines

- *Interoperability of the Metaverse: A Digital Ecosystem Perspective Review* (2025), IEEE Engineering Management Review. DOI: 10.1109/EMR.2025.3564923. Reports 74 relevant publications and uses technological, data, human, and institutional interoperability dimensions.
- *Metaverse Applications: Challenges, Limitations and Opportunities - A Systematic Literature Review* (2025), Information and Software Technology. DOI: 10.1016/j.infsof.2025.107701. Uses Kitchenham methodology and reports 35 selected primary studies.
- *Metaverse security and privacy research: A systematic review* (2025), Computers & Security. DOI: 10.1016/j.cose.2025.104602. Covers 2013–2024.
- *Metaverse meets distributed machine learning: A contemporary review on the development with privacy-preserving concerns* (2025), ICT Express. DOI: 10.1016/j.icte.2025.04.008. Reports systematic investigation of more than 100 studies.
- *The (Un)sustainability of the Metaverse: A topic modeling analysis of social, economic, and environmental impacts* (2026), Technology in Society. DOI: 10.1016/j.techsoc.2025.103095. Uses a 236-publication corpus and identifies disciplinary fragmentation.
- *Metaverse for Sustainable Development in a bibliometric analysis and systematic literature review* (2024), Journal of Cleaner Production. DOI: 10.1016/j.jclepro.2024.140610. PRISMA-based sample of 66 studies and TCCR-based research-gap analysis.

## Initial inference to test

The closest literature contains strong broad and domain-specific reviews, but the initial scan has not yet identified a single review that jointly normalizes:

problem definition → algorithm/method → workload/dataset → baseline → metric → experimental environment → evidence maturity → reproducibility → cross-layer trade-off.

This is a candidate differentiator only. It must be tested against the expanded closest-review corpus before being stated as a contribution.
