# 2024 Resource-Allocation Benchmark Audit

Target: Comprehensive survey on resource allocation for edge-computing-enabled metaverse, Computer Science Review (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.cosrev.2024.100680.

## Publication-level finding

The review covers edge-computing-enabled Metaverse resource allocation, reports 35 resource-allocation strategies, and includes a comparison/benchmark of 19 algorithms. It therefore contradicts any claim that the literature contains no comparative algorithm benchmarking.

## Separate artifact questions

A published benchmark does not by itself establish a maintained public benchmark repository, reusable implementations for all algorithms, common public workloads, pinned dependencies, seeds, automated rerun workflow, containerized environment, independent reproduction, or coverage of newer post-2024 formulations. These require direct artifact verification.

Current artifact support status: unresolved.

## Novelty consequence

The project must not claim to be the first benchmark of Metaverse resource-allocation algorithms.

A potentially distinct contribution would require evidence for a reproducibility-normalized, reusable, versioned and extensible benchmark covering newer heterogeneous formulations. This remains a hypothesis until the artifact audit is complete.
